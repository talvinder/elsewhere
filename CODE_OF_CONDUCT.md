# Code of conduct

Elsewhere follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be direct, kind, and useful. Debate ideas and implementation choices without
attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized
conduct are not accepted in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and handled in proportion to their severity.
